D. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR OUR BUILD
  1. Move quality is the whole game. The top five sit at site ACPL 18-23 and CPL 8.7-11.9 cp with
  blunder rates under 1%. Our ladder history shows site ACPL 61 (mostly the v1 build); v2 games so
  far (site ACPL): R31 4, R32 23, R33 12, R34 7, R35 6. Target for the final build: site ACPL <= 18,
  CPL <= 9 cp.
  2. Time is the cheapest edge. The top five think 1.36-1.54 s per move (max 4.4-17.0 s) and leave
  16-42 s unused, mostly by spending a fixed 3-4% of the remaining clock. A budget that spends 2-3x
  that in the middlegame (about 3-5 s on moves 10-40, more after an evaluation swing) and keeps a 10
  s reserve buys one to two extra plies on every critical move without any risk of flagging; the 0.5
  s increment covers a long endgame on its own.
  3. Draw policy: the top five gave away 1 half points by repeating while ahead, but accepted 27
  level repetitions. So: positive contempt against everyone below the top ten (they hand out half
  points; see RepA in the summary), near-zero contempt against the top three when we are not better,
  and a repetition-seeking mode when we are worse against them: they will take it.
  4. There are no swindles at the top: 96 of 103 positions at +300 were converted. A game against
  them is decided by the first -300, so the evaluation must be right in the middlegame structures
  the curated set produces (Sicilian Closed, English Symmetrical, Ruy Lopez Closed, Petroff,
  Caro-Kann) rather than by endgame technique.
  5. Tactics only pay against the teams that blunder from level positions: Patzer 1.2 (5.8/1000),
  Fuzzydafool (4.1/1000), checkers (3.3/1000). Against class-A engines keep the position sound and
  let the time edge and the endgame do the work.
  6. Clock pressure works against test_bot (13 games under 5 s), Fuzzydafool (16 games under 5 s),
  Mate in One (6 games under 5 s): keep those games long and complicated, never simplify early
  against them.
  7. Openings: a shipped book is low value (231 curated positions, the top 40 cover a third of
  games, and every game leaves theory within a few moves). What matters is a cheap, correct first
  move out of the curated position (a 1-2 s search is fine) and an evaluation that understands
  closed Sicilian, symmetrical English and closed Ruy Lopez structures.
