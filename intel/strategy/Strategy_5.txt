D. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR OUR BUILD
  1. Move quality is the whole game. The top five sit at site ACPL 16-21 and CPL 8.1-11.5 cp with
  blunder rates under 1%. Our ladder history shows site ACPL 38 (mostly the v1 build); v2 games so
  far (site ACPL): R31 4, R32 23, R33 12, R34 7, R35 6, R36 35, R37 7, R38 7, R39 5, R40 44, R41 7,
  R42 3, R43 25, R44 10, R45 26. Target for the final build: site ACPL <= 16, CPL <= 8 cp.
  2. Time is the cheapest edge. The top five think 1.3-1.53 s per move (max 4.4-17.0 s) and leave
  14-38 s unused, mostly by spending a fixed 3-4% of the remaining clock. A budget that spends 2-3x
  that in the middlegame (about 3-5 s on moves 10-40, more after an evaluation swing) and keeps a 10
  s reserve buys one to two extra plies on every critical move without any risk of flagging; the 0.5
  s increment covers a long endgame on its own.
  3. Draw policy: the top five gave away 1 half points by repeating while ahead, but accepted 36
  level repetitions. So: positive contempt against everyone below the top ten (they hand out half
  points; see RepA in the summary), near-zero contempt against the top three when we are not better,
  and a repetition-seeking mode when we are worse against them: they will take it.
  4. There are no swindles at the top: 112 of 119 positions at +300 were converted. A game against
  them is decided by the first -300, so the evaluation must be right in the middlegame structures
  the curated set produces (Sicilian Closed, English Symmetrical, Ruy Lopez Closed, Petroff,
  Caro-Kann) rather than by endgame technique.
  5. Tactics only pay against the teams that blunder from level positions: Patzer 1.2 (4.9/1000),
  checkers (3.2/1000), stockfih (2.3/1000). Against class-A engines keep the position sound and let
  the time edge and the endgame do the work.
  6. Clock pressure works against test_bot (19 games under 5 s), AI Fellow (25 games under 5 s),
  Mate in One (8 games under 5 s), stockfih (8 games under 5 s): keep those games long and
  complicated, never simplify early against them.
  7. Openings: a shipped book is low value (231 curated positions, the top 40 cover a third of
  games, and every game leaves theory within a few moves). What matters is a cheap, correct first
  move out of the curated position (a 1-2 s search is fine) and an evaluation that understands
  closed Sicilian, symmetrical English and closed Ruy Lopez structures.
