## 3. The conversion thesis — how losses and draws become wins

The directive is to convert non-wins into wins. The evidence says there are **exactly two levers**,
and neither is a contempt knob.

### 3.1 The loss was decided in the opening — fix the book

R98 left the book at **−70**, the worst starting evaluation of any game in the record, and declined
monotonically for eighty moves with **no blunder, no cliff, no time forfeit.** We were simply worse
from move five.

**Why:** the shipped book is **byte-identical across V6.5, V7k and V8** (md5 `51c97f3f…`), built
9 Sept 11:58 — before V7k existed. The net has been replaced twice since (+39, then +17). Measured
on the positions where the book actually fired in rounds 91–98, **it plays a different move than the
current net would choose in 12 of 22 cases — 55% disagreement.**

In R98 specifically, **three of four book moves were moves this net rejects**, and the position
walked from **+22 to −46** while the book was driving.

**We are playing our openings with an engine that no longer exists.** Fixing this is how a −70 start
becomes a +20 start, and how R98-shaped losses become draws or wins.

### 3.2 The endgame is played three plies shallower than the middlegame — fix the clock

Forensic sweep of every sharp evaluation drop across rounds 91–98: **every single shallow search
that preceded a collapse is in R98, in the endgame, under 12 seconds.**

| game | move | eval drop | depth | median depth | time spent | clock left |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R94 | 33 | 355cp | 17 | 18 | 1490 ms | 45.7 s |
| R96 | 36 | 284cp | 24 | 21 | 3872 ms | 56.2 s |
| R97 | 27 | 136cp | 19 | 18 | 3816 ms | 64.0 s |
| **R98** | **73** | 127cp | **16** | 20 | **529 ms** | **11.5 s** |
| **R98** | **79** | 177cp | **17** | 20 | **942 ms** | **9.6 s** |
| **R98** | **81** | 603cp | **17** | 20 | **575 ms** | **9.1 s** |

**The cause is structural**, in `agent.py::_budget`:

```python
mtg = max(18.0, 34.0 - 0.4 * move_number)
```

From move 40 onward `mtg` is **pinned at 18** — the engine assumes eighteen moves always remain. It
spends **1/18th of its remaining clock every move, front-loaded**, so by move 73 of an 89-move game
it is on 11.5 s playing at 0.5–1.0 s, **at depth 16–17 instead of its usual 20.**

Our game lengths: 43, 51, 52, 52, 53, 61, 71, 81, 83, 95, 98, 104, 106. **Long games are normal, and
in every one of them we under-resource the endgame.** R92 was won by a king march from **−17cp** —
endgame technique is a demonstrated strength of this engine, and we are starving it.

**This is the draw-conversion lever.** Depth in long endings is what turns a held draw into a win
and a lost ending into a held draw.

Note the direction: I already tested **MIDGAME_BOOST** (spend *more* early) — **+11 ±46, null**. The
evidence says the opposite: **reserve more for late.**

### 3.3 What the draws actually were — and why contempt is not the answer

| game | termination | peak eval | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| R87, R88, R90 | insufficient material | +18, +70, +84 | **genuinely level, correctly drawn** |
| R86 | threefold | +164 early, then −20 to −60 for 30 moves | **a save from a worse position** |
| R94 | threefold | +335 | **evaluation was wrong, the move was right** |

R94's +335 is a **transposition-table artefact**, reproduced exactly: warm table **+383** at depth 16,
cold table **−20** at depth 15, same position, same net. But warm or cold, at 1.5 s or 60 s, **the
engine plays Ke2 either way.** A wrong number, not a wrong move.

**Contempt is closed** across four instruments and ~4,000 games: 0 and 20 both score +68 vs s48; 45
scores +46; draw-seek costs 12; and at a real clock against a weaker opponent contempt 45 is **14 Elo
worse** than the shipped 20. **The shipped value is already optimal.** Three separate mechanisms —
static-eval anti-simplification, drawishness, and the search's terminal draw score — have all been
tried and all are dead. **Draws are converted by playing better, not by refusing to draw.**

## 6. Deliberately not attempted, with reasons

- **Re-deriving the 11 search constants.** Needs 11 gates. The one we did re-derive against the
  current net — the aspiration window — read **+14 on the screen and −3 at the clock.** Chasing the
  rest would be shipping screen numbers, which is how games get lost.
- **Retraining the net.** The lever is exhausted: recipe closed, width closed, from-scratch on
  depth-10 impossible before the lock on epoch arithmetic, endgame blending monotonically negative.
- **Fixing the TT/repetition interaction in the search.** Real bug, but engine-internals surgery on a
  build that is 41 rounds unbeaten, with no evidence it changes a move. Filed for after the lock.

## 7. Validation discipline

- **Every candidate gated at 120+0.5 through `arena_par` against V8** — the real wrapper, book on.
  `node_gate` cannot test `MIDGAME_BOOST`-class constants (its `_budget` is a module global applying
  to both sides) and its fixed-node mode inflates eval gains 2–3×.
- **Report the W/D/L shape, not only Elo.** The directive is conversion, so every gate reports
  **decisive rate and loss rate** alongside the rating — that is what "draws into wins" actually
  measures.
- **Two samples on anything close.** Two identical 400-game gates on fc_b returned **+23 and +1**.
- **Combination gate before shipping.** V7k's parts summed to +47 and measured +39.
- **Zero fallback games.** An arena that blows `agent.py`'s compile join plays on the fallback engine
  and prints a plausible, meaningless 50%. `BINI_JOIN_S=300`, and the fallback count is checked.
- **Final build verified:** `diff -r` against V8, init time, unzipped size, legal moves on the test
  suite, md5 recorded.

## 9. Honest expectation

The book fix is a **confirmed** defect repair; I expect it to be real but I will not guess its size
before the gate. The `mtg` fix is a **well-evidenced hypothesis** with a mechanism and a measurement
behind it, not a hunch. Together they attack exactly the two things that produced our only recent
loss: **starting behind, and finishing shallow.**

What I will not claim is that V8.5 stops us losing games. We are playing peers at 2950; R98 was lost
to an opponent we had beaten five rounds earlier, with no blunder in the game. **What is achievable
is a build with no known defect, whose openings are chosen by the engine that plays them, and whose
endgame runs at full depth.** That is what this plan delivers.
